An explicit division of responsibility

Models interpret.

Draft, summarize, extract, and classify. Model output can be wrong and needs task-appropriate validation.

Software enforces.

Validate fields, calculate values, qualify against criteria, and route work using repeatable rules.

People decide.

Set objectives, review exceptions, and own the decisions that require accountability.
