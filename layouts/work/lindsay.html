Lindsay Clancy Trial Intelligence is an independent DCD research project exploring the organization of a complex public record.

Keep the source
connected to the claim.

The project explores how transcripts, public records, timelines, and references can become an organized research interface.

Its broader design question is how structured information can support review without flattening conflicting evidence into a single narrative.

Experimental capability

Case intelligence

Analytical scoring and proposition-testing features are experimental. They are not validated predictions, findings of fact, or legal advice.
